Behind the camera since 2019.

“Raw, authentic-yet-controlled visuals and environmental storytelling.”

Luca is a Boston-based photographer and filmmaker. He was born in the United Kingdom, grew up in Maine, and started shooting in 2019 while working in marketing for Maine Media Workshops & College, where a long obsession with visual storytelling turned into a working craft. He moved to Boston in 2024.

Off set he travels, and on weekends he watches Arsenal.
